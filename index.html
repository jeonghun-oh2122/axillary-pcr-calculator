Model

Multivariable logistic regression in 258 patients with cN2–3 breast cancer who completed NAC and breast and axillary surgery at SNUBH, 2016–2020 (axillary pCR 49.3% of the full cohort of 276). Apparent AUC 0.90 for Model 3 and 0.82 for Model 2.

Reduction ratio = (pre-NAC − post-NAC) / pre-NAC × 100, floored at 0%. Lymph node size uses the short axis.

Use

For research and education. The model is internally developed and not externally validated, so it should not by itself decide whether to omit axillary dissection.
